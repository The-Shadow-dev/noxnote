Privacy

NoxNote for iPad and iPhone · As of November 2026
In short: NoxNote collects no data. No account, no ads, no analytics, no server run by the developer. Your notes stay on your device – and, if you turn it on, in your own iCloud Drive.

What stays on your device

Notebooks, pages, handwriting, images, PDFs, recordings, transcripts, questions and settings are stored by NoxNote only in the app’s storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data too. Once a day NoxNote puts a backup into its “Backups” folder in the Files app, also on your device (you can switch this off). You can also create a PDF, an image or a NoxNote file at any time through “Save and share”.

Microphone and speech recognition

The microphone is only used when you start a recording, a dictation or a transcript, and only after you have allowed it. Speech is recognised by Apple’s speech recognition. NoxNote asks for on-device recognition wherever the device offers it for the language. If your device does not offer that for a language, Apple may run the recognition on its own servers; Apple’s privacy policy applies to that.

Camera

The camera is only used when you put a photo on a page or scan a worksheet, and only after you have allowed it. Photos and scans stay in the notebook on your device as images.

Handwriting

Your handwriting is read by Apple’s text recognition (Vision) right on the device, to make it searchable, turn it into typed text or find labels for image questions. Nothing leaves the device in the process.

Links and videos

When you paste a link, NoxNote loads the image or the page’s preview image from that address and stores it in the notebook. In doing so the operator of that site sees your IP address, as with any visit from a browser. An embedded YouTube video first shows only its still image, which comes from i.ytimg.com (a YouTube server). It loads the player from youtube-nocookie.com only when you tap play. YouTube belongs to Google (USA); Google’s terms and privacy policy apply.

Study reminder

If you switch on the daily study reminder, NoxNote schedules the notifications on the device, after you have allowed them. There is no push service and no server for this: the app works out the number of due questions itself.

iCloud

If you turn on “Back up and sync with iCloud” (NoxNote asks once; you can change it in Settings), NoxNote puts your notebooks with their images, PDFs and recordings into its folder in your iCloud Drive. That way they are backed up and the same on your devices signed in with the same Apple ID. Apple stores and transfers them; Apple’s terms and privacy policy apply. The developer has no access to your iCloud Drive. If you turn it off, whatever is already in iCloud stays there until you delete it yourself.

Buying NoxNote

Apple handles the purchase through the App Store. The developer receives sales figures from Apple, but no details about you. There are no in-app purchases.

Sharing

When you share a notebook, images or questions, you decide in Apple’s share sheet where they go.

If you write to us

If you write to stilus.app@proton.me, the developer processes your email address and your message in order to reply. The mailbox is hosted by Proton (Proton AG, Switzerland). The message is deleted once the matter is closed, unless a legal retention duty applies.

This page on the web

In the app this page comes from the app package, without any request over the network. In a browser it is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). GitHub processes your IP address and technical details to deliver and protect the page. The developer does not analyse this.

Legal bases and your rights

Where personal data arises in the steps described here, the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (you want the feature or a reply) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (a legitimate interest in a secure, available page). With Apple, Google and GitHub, data may reach the USA. For the USA there is an adequacy decision of the European Commission (EU-US Data Privacy Framework of 10 July 2023); it covers companies that take part in it. How these companies transfer data to the USA is set out in their privacy policies.

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Write to the address below for this. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority. The developer can neither see nor change your notebooks: they are only with you and, if you turn it on, in your iCloud Drive.

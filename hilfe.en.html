NoxNote help

The most common questions. Not here? Write to stilus.app@proton.me.

How do my notes become questions?

Write two or more lines like “Osmosis: water moves through a membrane” and shortly afterwards NoxNote suggests questions from them at the bottom: one tap on “Create” and they are there. At any time you can also go to the top right of a notebook, “…” → Questions → “Make questions for me …” → “From what I have written”: NoxNote picks up such lines, vocabulary in two columns, and a question with its answer on the line below. Single questions go in with “New question”. Selected text becomes a question with “Question” in the bar above the selection.

How do I quiz myself on a sketch or a slide?

With image questions. Pick the drawing with the lasso and tap “Image question”, or tap “Image question” in the question panel and drag a frame, over a PDF slide too. NoxNote covers typed labels by itself, and in the app handwritten and printed ones as well. You drag further covers with your finger or the pen. Every cover becomes a question: while you are quizzed, all the spots are covered and the one being asked is marked in blue. With “Add a label” you give a cover its text, and then the question also works with multiple choice and typing.

When do questions come back?

What you don’t know comes back right away and again tomorrow. What you know comes back later each time: after 1, 3, 7, 14, 30 and 60 days. A question is due from the morning of its day, whatever time you studied. Before an exam, “Practise” under “Study” takes you through them, the shaky ones first.

What does NoxNote cost?

Pay once in the App Store, and everything is included. The App Store shows the price. No subscription, no in-app purchases, nothing to buy later. Family Sharing covers your whole family group, on all your devices.

May I record a class or a lecture?

Only if everyone who can be heard agrees, so ask first, even if you only let NoxNote transcribe. In Germany, recording words that are not spoken publicly without permission can be a criminal offence (section 201 of the Criminal Code). Other countries have their own rules, and so do many schools and universities. That is your responsibility.

Where do I find my recordings?

In the notebook, at the top under the speaker. From there you play them back or convert them with “To text”. The text then sits in the transcript.

My handwriting is recognised badly.

Writing a little bigger and leaving room between the lines helps most. Print is recognised better than tight cursive. Recognition runs on the device and needs no internet.

How do I back up my notes?

In a notebook, “…” → Save and share: as a PDF, as images, or with “Save notebook” as a NoxNote file that can be opened again later. You share questions in the question panel under “…”, for Anki too.

How do I write small and still neatly?

In a notebook, “…” → Write big. A large box appears at the bottom and a blue frame on the page. What you write big in the box lands small in the frame. If you write into the right edge of the box, the frame moves along, and at the end of a line it drops to the next one. You move it with your finger in the box or right at the frame; “Smaller” and “Bigger” above the box set the magnification, and “Done” closes it.

How do I find something in a long notebook?

In a notebook, “…” → Search this notebook, or ⌘F with a keyboard. NoxNote searches typed text, mind maps and the text of PDF slides, and in the app your handwriting too. Matches are marked on the page, and the arrows or the Enter key jump to the next one. You search across all notebooks in the library, where you will also find questions. In the sidebar under “Contents” you get a PDF’s table of contents and your bookmarks: press and hold a page in the bar and pick “Set a bookmark”.

Which gestures are there?

Press and hold a button to see what it does. Holding the pen still on the page selects. Tapping with two fingers undoes, three fingers redo. Dragging with two fingers zooms and pans. A double tap or a squeeze on the Apple Pencil follows your iPad settings.

How do I switch quickly between two notebooks?

As soon as you have a second notebook open, a slim bar appears under the header with your open notebooks – one tap switches, with no detour through the library. The dot shows the colour of the cover. The × only removes the tab; the notebook stays in the library. With a keyboard: Ctrl + 1 to Ctrl + 5 jumps to the tab in that position, Ctrl + Tab moves on (with Shift, back).

What happens when the page is full?

Just keep writing down to the bottom edge: NoxNote puts the next page underneath by itself, like a writing pad. The view stays where it is. Undo takes the page away again. You can also do it by hand – below the last page there is a “New page” area. In a PDF no page is added: there the pages belong to the document.

Where do I see when my next exam is?

Set a date in the notebook: “…” → Questions → Exam. After that the countdown is in the library under “Study” (“Exam in 4 days”, with “Practise” for every question in that notebook), and in every planner – month, week and day page – the date sits in the day as a coloured chip, in the colour of the notebook cover.
